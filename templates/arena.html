Flask + SQLite + Jinja Integration Contract

Presentation is isolated from data. To connect this read-only console to your Flask + SQLite backend, serialize your battle rows in Python and embed them into the Jinja template or pass a JSON endpoint URL. 

<!-- In templates/dashboard.html -->
<script id="arena-payload" type="application/json">
  {{ arena_payload | tojson }}
</script>

Expected top-level keys: mode ("historical_demo" | "live_archive"), status ("ready" | "empty" | "loading" | "error"), activeBattleId, battles[], and error.
